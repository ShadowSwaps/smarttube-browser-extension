# Compact player tools: 0.4.8 preview

The bounded keyboard/layout candidate passed coordinator non-author acceptance on 19 October (one pass, no repairs). The separate 0.4.8 release/source/package review and publication verification remain pending. Published 0.4.7 and its accepted evidence remain preserved. No normal-profile installation or acceptance is claimed for 0.4.8.

SmartTube adds one **ST** entry to YouTube's native right control bar on regular players. It opens a compact tools panel with **+ Queue**, **Next** and **Cinema** quick actions. **Loop video / A–B** expands whole-video looping, range endpoints and Clear. **Picture and distractions** expands optional picture/hide tools. Undo skip appears only after a skip, with an availability indicator on ST.

YouTube owns play/pause, seeking, volume/mute and current speed. Their duplicate SmartTube buttons have been removed from the player and popup. The subsequent [0.4.6 preview](STARTUP_VOLUME.md) starts new/reloaded videos at normal 1× and adds native speed synchronization with readable achieved-speed feedback. Editable saved startup speed controls are retired; old stored fields remain unused. Alt-wheel volume has brief percentage feedback; Alt-Shift-wheel speed uses0.05× steps within the native player’s supported limits. Startup volume defaults to5% with deliberate verified player unmute, and wheel controls start enabled; explicit volume/wheel opt-outs remain respected. Native startup/quality implementation and the 0.4.7 release passed their required reviews; historical checkpoints remain in validation.

The same entry moves to a subdued top-right fallback when the native control bar is absent, the player is narrower than 640px, or native chrome is hidden. Integrated and fallback entries are never intentionally shown together. The fallback remains visible and in normal Tab order during native auto-hide, so reaching tools needs no pointer prerequisite. It does not force native controls to stay visible. An open tools panel remains independent of auto-hide; moving the entry preserves focus inside its tools.

Use mouse/touch or focus ST and press Enter/Space. Opening focuses Close, then Tab reaches Queue and the remaining tools. Escape outside fullscreen closes tools and returns focus to ST; another Escape can exit Cinema. Outside clicks or focus leaving the tools close them without pulling focus back. Tools do not trap focus or take over site inputs. In native fullscreen, SmartTube leaves Escape to the browser; automated fullscreen checks use actual native entry/exit, but do not establish a universal browser-level Escape guarantee.

The popover scrolls within the player above the bottom controls and detected caption area. A side gutter separates the fallback entry from the entire scrolling panel, including expanded Picture controls. Focused controls scroll into view; keyboard Undo moves focus to Close when its button disappears. Picture tools in the player are explicitly **for this tab**: they change presentation without writing saved preferences. Reloading clears these overrides. Changed saved preferences from Settings/popup supersede the corresponding tab override. **Reset video filters** restores neutral filtering for the tab; persistent defaults remain in extension Settings. Popup/Settings picture controls continue to save locally as before.

The Brave popup prioritizes current-video context and the queue, with grouped Loop/Cinema and Picture tools plus Settings access. Queue actions remain available without an active player; player-specific actions are disabled when no video is connected or enhancements are off. This panel does not launch the browser action popup.

Implementation uses original isolated-world DOM/CSS and HTML media APIs. Native control DOM is site-dependent, not a public integration API. It does not replace native settings, inject internal player code, add permissions, change queue tracker/background/receipt/storage semantics, or bundle third-party implementation/assets. Future YouTube layouts and arbitrary third-party configurations remain compatibility limits.
